## 📌 About

This presentation explores some of the major **paradoxes and challenges in India's power system**, including the growing renewable-energy capacity, continued dependence on thermal generation, DISCOM financial problems, and India's dependence on imported solar and energy-storage technologies.

---

## ⚡ Topics Covered

* 🇮🇳 Current and future Indian power-grid scenario
* ☀️ Solar + thermal generation paradox
* 🔋 Capacity vs. actual electricity generation
* 💰 DISCOM debt and the cross-subsidy problem
* 🏠 Utility-scale solar vs. rooftop solar
* 🌏 Dependence on imported solar cells and materials
* 🔌 Imported inverters and power electronics
* 🔋 Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) dependency

The presentation highlights India's installed capacity and future grid plans, including renewable expansion, energy storage, HVDC corridors, and smart-grid development.

It also discusses the financial condition of DISCOMs and the challenges associated with India's electricity-distribution sector.

---

## 🔍 Key Idea

India is rapidly increasing renewable generation capacity, but the power system still faces challenges involving **generation availability, grid balancing, distribution finances, technology imports, and energy storage**.

The presentation examines these issues through three major paradoxes:

1. **Capacity Duplication Paradox**
2. **DISCOM "Death Spiral" & Cross-Subsidy Collapse**
3. **Utility-Scale Solar vs. Rooftop Solar**
